# Account & Data Deletion Request
## Nayaka Samaj Directory App

We respect your right to control your personal data.

If you wish to delete your account and associated data from the Nayaka Samaj Directory app, please follow the instructions below.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and personal data, please send an email with the following details:

**Email:** uvnayka@gmail.com  
**Subject:** Account Deletion Request – Nayaka Samaj Directory

Please include:
- Your registered mobile number or email address
- Your full name (as used in the app)
- Reason for deletion (optional)

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification, we will permanently delete:
- Your user account
- Profile information
- Contact details
- Any associated community or business data linked to your account

---

## Data Retention

Some minimal data may be retained if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as audit or security logs), and will be handled securely.

---

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are usually processed within **7–14 working days**.

---

If you have any questions, feel free to contact us at the email above.
