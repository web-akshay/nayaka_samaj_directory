# Child Safety Standards
## Nayaka Samaj Directory App

Nayaka Samaj Directory is a community-based application intended for adult members of the Nayaka Samaj.

We are committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment and take child safety seriously.

---

## Age Restriction

This app is intended for users **18 years and above**.

We do not design features for children, and we do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or use the app.

---

## Protection of Minors

To help protect minors:
- User registration is required to access app features
- The app does not include chat, messaging, or direct user-to-user communication
- The app does not allow public content posting or commenting
- No inappropriate, sexual, violent, or harmful content is promoted

---

## Reporting Concerns

If you believe any content or user activity violates child safety standards, or if you have concerns related to child safety, please contact us immediately.

**Contact Email:** support@yourdomain.com  
**Subject:** Child Safety Concern – Nayaka Samaj Directory

All reports are reviewed seriously and appropriate action is taken.

---

## Compliance

Nayaka Samaj Directory complies with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards and related policies.

We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users.
